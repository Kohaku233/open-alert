A notification channel is where your notifications land, such as the Pushover app on your phone or a Discord channel you choose. Each account can add up to 10 channels.

## Add a channel

On the **Notifications** page, click **Add channel** and pick a channel type. Give it a name you will recognize (1 to 40 characters), then fill in the settings for that type.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Pushover">
    1. Install Pushover on your phone and sign in.
    2. Open your account page on the Pushover website and copy your **User Key** (30 letters or digits).
    3. Paste it into **User Key**.
    4. You can leave **Personal application token** empty. If you do, the site's shared token is used. To use your own, create one on Pushover's application page and paste it here.

    When you save, the system checks your key with Pushover. It does not send a test notification at this step.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Discord">
    1. In Discord, open the channel's **Settings → Integrations → Webhooks**.
    2. Create a new webhook and copy its URL.
    3. Paste it into **Discord Webhook URL**.

    The Discord app controls its own notification sounds, so the sound settings here do not apply to it.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="caution" title="New channels receive nothing by default">
After you add a channel, go to [Delivery rules](/en/notify/routes/), turn on the messages it should receive, and click **Save routing**.
</Aside>

## Test

Click **Test** next to a channel to send a test notification at normal priority. You can test the same channel once every 30 seconds. Test messages also count toward that day's delivery attempts.

## Disable, edit and delete

- **Disable**: Turn off the switch next to the channel. A disabled channel receives nothing, even if it is turned on in your delivery rules.
- **Edit**: You can change the channel name or update the key or webhook URL. If you leave a key field empty, the saved value is kept. If you change your Pushover personal application token, you must enter your User Key again too.
- **Delete**: Removes the channel and its delivery rules for good. You cannot undo this.

## Coming soon

Telegram, DingTalk, WeCom and QQ are planned. They are not yet available in the channel type list.
