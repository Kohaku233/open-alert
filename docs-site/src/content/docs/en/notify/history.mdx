---
title: Delivery history
description: Check the results of your recent notifications and what each status means.
---

**Delivery history** at the bottom of the Notifications page shows your 100 most recent notifications. Records are kept for 30 days. Click **Refresh history** to get the latest delivery status.

## What each result means

| Result | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sent | The channel received it | If you didn't see it, check your phone's Focus mode and the app's notification permissions |
| Skipped | Blocked by quiet hours or a daily limit, or the channel isn't fully set up | Check your delivery rules and channel settings. Daily limits reset after 00:00 UTC |
| Failed | The channel clearly rejected it | Check whether your key or webhook is still valid |
| Delivery uncertain | Something like a network drop means we don't know if it arrived | Check the receiving end first, so you don't act twice |

"Sent" only means the channel accepted the request. It does not mean your device showed it right away or that you read it.

Each channel in the channel list also shows its latest delivery result, so you can quickly spot problems with a specific channel.
