The **Settings** page shows your account details and usage in one place.

## Account details

- Your email and name.
- The sign-in methods available to you.

## Beta access and usage

During the beta, every invited and enabled account gets Pro features free of charge. This section shows how much you are using:

- Number of price alerts
- Number of market slots
- Number of notification channels
- Number of asset favorites
- Price alert delivery attempts today

Click **Refresh usage** to get the latest numbers.

## Appearance and language

- **Appearance**: System, Dark or Light.
- **Interface language**: Chinese or English.

These preferences are saved in your current browser.
