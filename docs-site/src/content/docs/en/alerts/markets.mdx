Only USDT-quoted markets are supported.

## Search for an asset

In the search box at the top of the Prices page, you can enter a coin name, a trading pair, or the exchange's own symbol, such as `ETH`, `ETHUSDT`, or `ETH-USDT-SWAP`.

- Results are grouped by asset. Each asset lists every exchange and market where it trades.
- You can filter by exchange and market type.
- If there are too many results, only the first ones are shown. Try a more specific search term.
- Grayed-out markets marked "Unavailable" can't be selected for now. This usually means the exchange delisted the market or it's under maintenance.

## Favorites

Click the star next to an asset to add the whole asset to your favorites. This makes it quicker to switch between exchanges later. Each account can favorite up to 50 assets.

## What if a saved market is delisted?

The alert keeps its original market, and the app shows a notice that the original market is unavailable. You can:

- keep adjusting the alert's other settings, or pause the alert;
- to enable it again, switch to an available market first, then save.

The system never switches markets for you.
