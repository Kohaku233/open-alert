## Alert states

| State | Meaning | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armed | Waiting for the price to meet the condition | Edit, pause, delete |
| Notification queued / Sending | Triggered, and the notification is on its way | Wait for it to finish. You can't change the alert during this time |
| Triggered | The notification has been sent and the alert is locked | Rearm, edit, delete |
| Delivery uncertain | The notification may or may not have arrived | Check your phone first, then decide whether to rearm |

## Rearm

An alert that has triggered only becomes active again after you click **Rearm**. Changing the price or toggling the alert on or off does not unlock it. This keeps a parameter tweak from sending you a notification by accident.

## Monitoring switch

The monitoring switch controls every price alert in your account:

- **Off**: no alert can trigger, and any queued notifications that haven't gone out yet are cancelled. Requests already sent to a channel can't be recalled.
- **On**: price checks resume. As soon as the first fresh price meets a condition, that alert triggers.

<Aside>
The monitoring switch only controls price alerts. Anomaly monitoring has its own switch, and the two don't affect each other.
</Aside>

## Delete an alert

A deleted alert disappears from your list, but its delivery history is still kept in the background. If an alert is queued or sending, you can delete it once sending finishes.
